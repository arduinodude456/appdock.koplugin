# AppDock - TBS Account user data privacy policy
## What data is TBS collecting?
- Settings/Configuration, excluding personal information like Profile Picture, Username
- Recent used apps
## Why do we store this data
- To give younthe ability of migrating settings between eReaders
- Improving user experience
## How long do we store your data
- up to 7 months, delete request possible
## How you can access your data
- View the logs in settings.reader.lua
