# AppDock 6.2.2 — Android Keyboard and Switch Cosmetics

AppDock 6.2.2 adds an AppDock-owned Android-style on-screen keyboard to the AppDock homescreen app search. The keyboard is composed from stable KOReader primitives and supports letters, backspace, spaces, clear and done.

The standard KOReader dialogs remain in place everywhere else, including AppDock manager, browser, DApp and lockscreen flows. The Quick Settings Wi-Fi, Night Mode and Power Saving tiles now use a clearer Material-style switch with a rounded track and an offset circular thumb rendered through the existing stable `QuickTile` and `FixedStack` path.
